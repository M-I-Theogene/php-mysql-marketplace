
CYPADI
KYC &amp; AML Policy
Version 1.0
Effective Date: July 2026
Purpose
Cypadi is committed to preventing fraud and complying with applicable anti-money laundering requirements.
This policy applies to customers and vendors using the Cypadi Shop marketplace in Rwanda.
Customer and Vendor Identification
Basic information such as name, email, phone number, and country may be collected during registration or checkout.
Where necessary, we may request identification from customers or vendors before processing transactions, enabling payouts, or approving high-risk activity.
Risk Assessment
We assess transactions and vendor activity based on patterns and may request additional information where necessary.
Transaction Monitoring
We monitor transactions for unusual or suspicious activity that may indicate fraud or misuse.
We reserve the right to delay, decline, or suspend transactions while investigations are ongoing.
Customer Payments and Vendor Payouts
Customer payments are collected for confirmed marketplace orders through approved payment methods, including mobile money.
Vendor payouts related to order fulfillment may be processed after successful completion of customer orders, in line with this policy.
Action on Suspicious Activity
If suspicious activity is identified, we may temporarily suspend related accounts or transactions and cooperate with relevant authorities where required by law.
Record Keeping
Relevant transaction and user information is stored securely for operational and business purposes.
Users and vendors may be asked to update their information if details change or if additional verification is required.
Policy Review
This policy may be updated as Cypadi's business operations and compliance requirements evolve.
