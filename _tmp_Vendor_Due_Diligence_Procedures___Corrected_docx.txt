
CYPADI
Vendor Due Diligence Procedures
Version 1.0
Effective Date: July 2026
Purpose
This document outlines the basic checks Cypadi performs before allowing use of the platform and related payment services.
Cypadi operates an e-commerce platform as the merchant of record for products sold to customers. For our current operations, this document describes checks applied to customers and platform users during registration, ordering, and payment.
Customer Registration
Before using the platform, we collect:
Full name
Email address
Phone number
Country of operation (collected during registration or at checkout when a delivery address is provided)
Identity Verification
Customers are required to provide the necessary information during onboarding or checkout. Where required, identification information may be requested to confirm identity before approving certain transactions or payment flows.
Accepted documents may include:
Government-issued ID
Additional supporting information if needed
Order Fulfillment
Cypadi operates an e-commerce platform where customers place orders and receive products after payment confirmation. Orders are fulfilled and delivered after successful payment.
Product and Service Review
We review the type of products offered on the platform to ensure they comply with our platform policies and applicable laws. Users offering illegal goods or services, or activities prohibited by our platform policies, will not be approved or may have access restricted.
Customer Payments
Customer payments are collected for confirmed product orders through approved payment methods, including mobile money where available. Any operational payments related to order fulfillment are handled separately from customer checkout, where applicable.
Monitoring
We monitor transactions and activity patterns and may investigate, delay, or suspend transactions if unusual or suspicious behavior is detected.
Record Keeping
Relevant transaction and user information is stored securely for operational and business purposes. Customers may be asked to update their information if details change or if additional verification is required.
Policy Updates
This document may be updated as Cypadi's operations and compliance requirements evolve.
