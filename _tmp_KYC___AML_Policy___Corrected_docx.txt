
CYPADI
KYC &amp; AML Policy
Version 1.0
Effective Date: July 2026
Purpose
Cypadi is committed to preventing fraud and complying with applicable anti-money laundering requirements. This policy applies to customers using our e-commerce platform to purchase products and make payments through approved payment methods.
Customer Identification
Where necessary, we may request identification from customers before processing transactions or payments. Identification may be requested during onboarding, checkout, or where a transaction requires additional review.
Risk Assessment
We assess transactions based on activity patterns and may request additional information where necessary.
Transaction Monitoring
We monitor transactions for unusual or suspicious activity that may indicate fraud or misuse.
We reserve the right to delay, decline, or suspend transactions while investigations are ongoing.
Customer Payments
Customer payments are collected for confirmed product orders through approved payment methods, including mobile money where available. Payments are processed only in connection with legitimate product orders placed on the platform.
Action on Suspicious Activity
If suspicious activity is identified, we may temporarily suspend related accounts or transactions and cooperate with relevant authorities where required by law.
Record Keeping
Relevant transaction and customer information is stored securely for operational and business purposes. Customers may be asked to update their information if details change or if additional verification is required.
Policy Review
This policy may be updated as Cypadi's business operations and compliance requirements evolve.
