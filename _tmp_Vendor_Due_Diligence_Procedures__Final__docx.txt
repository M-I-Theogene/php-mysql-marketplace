
CYPADI
Vendor Due Diligence Procedures
Version 1.0
Effective Date: July 2026
Purpose
This document outlines the checks Cypadi performs before allowing vendors (sellers) to use the marketplace and receive payments.
Cypadi Shop (shop.cypadi.com) is an online marketplace in Rwanda. Independent sellers list products; customers purchase and pay by mobile money.
Vendor Registration
Before a vendor is approved as a seller, we collect:
Full name
Email address
Phone number
Business name (if applicable)
Country of operation
Identity Verification
Vendors are required to provide necessary information during onboarding. Where required, identification may be requested before enabling payouts or high-value sales.
Accepted documents may include:
Government-issued ID
Business registration documents, where applicable
Additional supporting information if needed
Vendor Review
We review the type of products or services offered by vendors to ensure they comply with platform policies and applicable laws.
The sale of illegal goods or services, or activities prohibited by our platform policies, is not permitted. Vendor accounts may be suspended if issues are identified.
Order Fulfillment
Customers place orders on the marketplace and pay through approved payment methods. Orders are fulfilled after successful payment confirmation.
Payouts
Customer payments are collected for confirmed product orders through approved payment methods, including mobile money.
Cypadi may process payouts to vendors related to order fulfillment after successful completion of customer orders, where applicable.
Monitoring
We monitor transactions and vendor activity patterns and may investigate, delay, or suspend transactions if unusual or suspicious behavior is detected.
Record Keeping
Relevant vendor, transaction, and customer information is stored securely for operational and business purposes.
Vendors may be asked to update their information if details change or if additional verification is required.
Policy Updates
This document may be updated as Cypadi's operations and compliance requirements evolve.
